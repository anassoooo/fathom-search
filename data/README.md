## Source

Amazon product listings collected for the original VIO project
(see `docs/report.pdf`). Images are referenced by their Amazon CDN URLs.
The full CSV and the images are kept out of git. Only the small sample is
committed, so the project runs end to end from a fresh clone.

## Known data-quality issues (profiled 2026-10-10)

These affect search and evaluation design. Issues 1 and 2 are handled at ingest
([ADR-0002](../docs/adr/0002-dedup-and-category-aliases.md)): 42,427 rows become
**35,736 products** across **25 categories**.

1. **Duplicate listings.** 6,054 titles appear more than once (10,069 extra rows,
   24% of the catalog), usually the same item listed under several categories
   (e.g. a laptop under both *Electronics › Laptops* and *Gaming › Gaming laptops*).
   The largest group has 119 copies.
2. **Overlapping categories.** For example *Art & Craft* / *Arts & Crafts* / *Art Supplies*,
   and *Beauty & Personal* / *Beauty & Personal Care*.
3. **Brand-only titles.** 12.2% of names have two words or fewer, concentrated in
   Footwear (97% of the category) and Jewelry (92%). Text retrieval has little
   to work with for these, so image vectors matter.
4. **Price outliers.** The maximum is a $40,922 office chair, which is likely a data error.

## Trust: audit, contract, and handling policy

The catalog is treated as a trusted source only after `vio data audit` passes the
contract (`data/contract.toml`). Latest report: [reports/quality_report.md](reports/quality_report.md).

| Issue | Detection | Policy | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schema, nulls, invalid values, duplicate IDs | `schema.*`, `completeness.*`, `validity.*`, `uniqueness.product_id` | **Block**: the dataset is not ingested | Structural errors make every downstream number wrong |
| Duplicate listings | `uniqueness.duplicate_listings` | **Merge** at ingest (title + price + image ID) | One item should be one result ([ADR-0002](../docs/adr/0002-dedup-and-category-aliases.md)) |
| Overlapping categories | profiling | **Alias map** to canonical names; raw values kept | Filters must not miss sibling categories (ADR-0002) |
| Wrong Bayesian prior (m = median price) | `consistency.bayes_rating_source` | **Recompute** with empirical-Bayes prior; source kept as `*_source` | Method error ([ADR-0003](../docs/adr/0003-empirical-bayes-rating-prior.md)) |
| Category disagreement (title vs category) | `labels.category_disagreement` | **Flag + add** the suggested category; never remove listed ones | Manual audit: 72.5% taxonomy overlap, 12.5% real errors, 15% classifier wrong |
| Price outlier (robust z > 3.5 and >= 3x median) | `plausibility.price_outlier` | **Flag**; keep searchable | Mostly genuine luxury or pro items (Rolex, $2.8K press tool); a few errors ($40,922 chair). Price alone can't tell them apart |
| Low evidence (< 5 reviews) | `plausibility.low_evidence_rating` | **Flag**; the Bayesian rating already shrinks it | Raw rating is unreliable |
| Brand-only title | `text.brand_only_title` | **Flag**; rely on image vectors | Too little text signal |
| Mojibake, HTML entities, truncation | `text.*` | **Flag** | Rare (≤ 56 rows); not worth guessing a fix |

**Downstream rules:**
- Evaluation (M2) draws seed products only from **unflagged** products, so test queries
  aren't built on doubtful data.
- Search can exclude flags with a Qdrant filter on `quality_flags`.
- Nothing is silently dropped.
